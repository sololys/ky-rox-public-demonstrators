# Contributing

All contributions must retain zero external runtime dependencies, pass existing deterministic gates, and carry a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO 1.1) sign-off (`git commit -s`).

By adding `Signed-off-by: Name <email>` to your commit, you certify under [DCO 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/) that you authored the patch or hold the legal right to submit it under this repository's license.

Pull requests without `Signed-off-by`, introducing IEEE 754 floating-point into gating paths, or breaking clean-clone test execution (< 10 s) fail closed at review.

Human sovereignty (`Level 01`) governs merge authority; automated agents hold `0.00 %` legal capacity.
